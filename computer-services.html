Most single-device repairs — screens, batteries, virus removal — are completed same-day. Complex issues like motherboard repair or data recovery can take 24–48 hours.

Yes — we offer in-shop drop-off, on-site visits for businesses and networking jobs, and remote support for many software issues.

It won't. We diagnose first and get your approval on a flat-rate quote before any repair work begins.
