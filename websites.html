Websites

Build a website with AI — step by step

You don't need to know how to code. Describe what you want, and today's AI website tools will design, write, and build it for you. Here's the exact process, a prompt to start from, and the tools we recommend.

The process

  1. Write down what your site is for

    One or two sentences: who it's for and the single action a visitor should take (call you, book, buy, read). Everything else flows from this.

  2. Gather your raw material

    Your business name, a few photos, your services or products with prices, contact details, and 2–3 sites you like the look of. AI fills gaps, but real details make it yours.

  3. Pick a tool below and paste the starter prompt

    Start with our Best Choice unless the "why" on another pick fits you better. Paste the prompt, replace the bracketed parts with your details, and generate.

  4. Refine in plain English

    Don't settle for the first draft. Ask for changes the way you'd tell a designer: "make the header darker", "add a testimonials section", "shorter about text".

  5. Connect a domain and publish

    Every tool below has a publish step — connect your own domain name (or use a free subdomain to start) and your site is live.

Your starter prompt

Copy, fill the [brackets], paste into the tool 
Build a modern, mobile-friendly website for [business name], a [type of business] in [city].

Pages: Home, About, Services, Contact.

The main goal: get visitors to [call us / book an appointment / buy online].

Style: clean and professional, [dark/light] theme, accent color [color]. 

Include: a clear headline about [main service], a services section with [list your services and prices], customer reviews section, and a contact section with [phone/address/hours].

Write all the text in simple, friendly English. Avoid buzzwords.
