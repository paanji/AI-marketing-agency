AI video tools have gone from gimmick to genuinely useful in the last year. Whether you're a content creator who needs to turn a script into footage, a marketer making product demos, or a student putting together a presentation, there's now an AI tool that can save you hours of manual editing.

Some tools here, like Runway and Sora, generate entire video clips from a text description. Others, like HeyGen and Synthesia, create realistic AI avatars that can present your script on camera without ever filming yourself. Tools like Kling AI and Pika focus on cinematic, high-fidelity video generation for creative projects.
