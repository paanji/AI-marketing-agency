Voice & Audio

Generate voiceovers and audio with AI — step by step

Voiceovers for videos, podcast intros, phone greetings, audiobook narration — typed as text, spoken back in a natural voice, in almost any language. Here's the process and the tools that sound genuinely human.

The process

  1. Write the script first

    Write it the way people actually speak — short sentences, contractions, no formal essay language. Read it aloud once; if you stumble, the AI voice will sound stiff there too.

  2. Choose the voice character

    Decide gender, age feel, accent, and energy (calm narrator vs upbeat ad read) before browsing voices — otherwise you'll scroll voice lists forever.

  3. Pick a tool below and paste your script

    Start with our Best Choice. Paste your script and preview 2–3 different voices against it.

  4. Fix pronunciation and pacing

    Names and local words often need help: spell them phonetically, add commas for pauses, or use the tool's emphasis controls.

  5. Export in the right format

    MP3 for most uses; WAV if it's going into video editing. Generate a spare take with slightly different pacing — future-you will thank you.

Your starter prompt

A script format that reads naturally — fill and paste 
[Write your script here in short, spoken sentences.]

Example:

Voice direction: [warm and friendly / professional narrator / energetic ad read], [language/accent], medium pace.
